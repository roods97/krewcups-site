Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Krew Cups is an app for riders and runners. You order your coffee before a club ride or run, and it's ready at the cafe when you get there. This policy explains what we collect about you, why, who we share it with, where it's kept, how long we keep it, and how to have it deleted.

"Krew Cups", "we" and "us" means Rudi Jooste, trading as Krew Cups, ABN 72 877 840 225, of Western Australia, Australia.

If you have a question, or want to see, correct or delete your data, email info@krewcups.com.

The short version

  • We collect your email, Pickup name, Usual, orders, and your Strava clubs and their events. Section 1
  • Your location is used only if you allow it, and tracked positions are deleted after 7 days. Section 1
  • Cafes see your Pickup name and drink. Never your email or where you are. Section 3
  • Stripe holds your card. We never see the number. Section 4
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads or to train AI. Section 3
  • Delete your account any time in Settings. It happens at once. Section 8

The full policy

1 What we collect Your email, Pickup name, Usual and orders. Your Strava clubs and their events. Your location only if you allow it. Stripe holds your card.

Your account

  • Your email address. You sign in with a one-time code sent to it. We also use it to email you about your account, for example to confirm that it's deleted, and to contact you about a problem reported with one of your orders.
  • Your Pickup name. The name your coffee is made under and written on your cup, such as "Sam K.". It starts as the first name and last initial from your Strava profile. We keep only that, not your full Strava name. You can change it at any time, and once you have, reconnecting Strava won't change it back.
  • Your Usual. The drink you normally have: its size, any changes (such as oat milk or an extra shot), whether you're having it in or taking it away, and the swaps you approve in advance for when the cafe can't make part of it.
  • Whether you're going. For each of your clubs' Events, whether you've said you're going or not going.
  • Your orders. The orders you place: which Event, which cafe, what you ordered, any note you add, the price, the platform fee and what happened to the order (made, collected, refunded and so on).
  • Problems you report. If you flag a problem with an order, we keep your reason, what you wrote, the photo you attached, and how you answered the cafe's response. Before your photo is uploaded, the app removes its hidden details, such as where it was taken.
  • Problems a cafe reports. A cafe can flag a problem with one of your orders, for example that it was never collected. We keep the cafe's reason and what it wrote. You aren't shown these reports, but you can ask us for a copy. If several of these are upheld, your account can be suspended from ordering. We keep a record of any suspension, and you're told why.

Cafes without Krew Cups ordering (Ad hoc Cafes)

If your Event has no cafe yet, you can ask for one to be added, giving its name and a Google Maps link, and you can tell us the prices on its menu. We keep that it was you who asked, and the prices you entered.

If you offer to place and pay for the group's order at such a cafe (you "Claim" it), we keep that you did. To receive the group's payments, you set up a payout account with Stripe. Stripe asks you directly for your identity and bank details, and keeps them under its own privacy policy. We hold only Stripe's reference to that account.

If a member's card can't be charged for such an order, Krew Cups may pay that member's share to the member who placed the order. We then keep a record of the amount the member owes us, until it's paid.

From Strava

Krew Cups needs a Strava account. When you connect it, you approve the connection on Strava's own page, and Strava sends us the data below. We don't get your Strava password.

  • Your Strava athlete ID, and the keys Strava gives us to read your data on your behalf. The long-lived key is stored encrypted in a separate, access-controlled vault, not in our ordinary tables.
  • Your name from your Strava profile, used only to set your starting Pickup name (see above).
  • Your Strava clubs: which clubs you belong to, and each club's name.
  • Your clubs' upcoming group events: the title, start time, meeting location, sport (ride or run), route distance and the route's line on the map. We use this to know when and where to expect you, and which cafe your order goes to.

Strava access we ask for: read only, which covers your public profile, your clubs and your clubs' events. We don't read your activities.

We check your club list with Strava at least every 6 days, and whenever you sync. Upcoming Events are refreshed from Strava daily.

We read your Strava data only to run Krew Cups. We don't sell it, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models. Your clubs' events are shown to other Krew Cups members in the same club, because they're the club's events, not yours.

Your location (only if you allow it)

Krew Cups uses your location only if you allow it in your phone's settings. With location permission off, we never use your location, and the app works out when you'll arrive from the group's pace instead.

With it allowed, we use it in two ways:

  • Before an Event (Location ping). About ten minutes before the start, your phone checks once whether you're near the start, so we can send you the order reminder at the right moment. With "Always" location permission, your phone can keep watching, until the start, for you arriving nearby. Your phone sends us that position, but we don't store it. We keep only your distance from the start.
  • During an Event, while you have an order (Live tracking). Your phone sends us its position every so often, so the cafe can have your coffee ready when you actually arrive. Tracking runs only while you have a live order. It stops when you arrive, when your order is finished or cancelled, 30 minutes after you were expected, or when you press Stop on the Event screen. For each position we store the latitude, longitude, accuracy, the time your phone took it and the time we received it.

We keep the times worked out from your positions: when your order was sent to the cafe, and when you arrived.

Your payment card

Your card is saved with, and charged by, Stripe. Your card number never reaches Krew Cups' own systems. We hold only Stripe's reference to you and to your card, and the amounts and results of your payments. See section 4.

Your phone

  • A push notification token, so we can send you order reminders and updates, plus a record of which notifications we sent you.
  • Your phone's location permission: whether you granted it.

If you register interest on krewcups.com

The form on our website collects your email address, whether you're a rider or runner, club organiser or cafe owner, and your club's or cafe's name if you give it. A cafe owner, here or on cafe.krewcups.com, also gives the cafe's name and their own name, and can add a phone number and suburb. We use it to email you (or, if you gave a phone number, call you) when Krew Cups launches near you, plus the odd product update. To unsubscribe, or have your details deleted, email info@krewcups.com, or reply "unsubscribe" to any of these emails. We act on it within 5 working days.

3 Who sees your data Cafes see your Pickup name and drink, never where you are. We don't sell your data.
  • Cafes see your Pickup name, your drink, when you're expected and a countdown, never where you are, your email address or your Strava data. If you flag an order, that cafe sees your reason, what you wrote and your photo.
  • Other members of your club see the club's Events. At an Ad hoc Cafe, the drink list for the group order shows no names. Once the group order is complete, the member who placed it sees each member's Pickup name and whether their payment went through.
  • Krew Cups (Rudi Jooste) can see the data in section 1 to run the app and look into problems.
  • The services we use, listed in section 5, only to run Krew Cups.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.
4 Payments Stripe charges your card. A Krew Cups cafe charges when it starts making your coffee; an Ad hoc order is a hold until the group order is paid.

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you save a card, you enter it into Stripe's own form, and Stripe keeps it. You have one saved card at a time.

At a cafe that takes Krew Cups orders, your card is charged when the cafe starts making your coffee. Until then you can change or cancel your order at no charge. The payment goes to the cafe's own Stripe account, and Krew Cups takes its platform fee from it. For each order, Stripe uses your card on the cafe's Stripe account once, and then that copy is removed. The cafe's record of the payment shows your card's brand and last 4 digits, as with any card payment.

At an Ad hoc Cafe, we place a hold on your card when you order. We take the payment when the member who placed the group order completes it, and pass it to them. If nobody completes it, the hold is released.

Stripe handles your card under its own privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy. We keep a record of each payment (amount, fee, status, Stripe's reference) for the cafe's and our payment and tax records.

6 How long we keep it Positions 7 days. Event details from Strava 7 days. Payment records 5 years after you delete your account, without your name.
  • Tracked positions: 7 days after tracking stops, then deleted. We keep them that long only to look into a problem you or the cafe reports.
  • Your distance from the start (Location ping): until you delete your account.
  • An Event's details from Strava (title, location, sport, route distance): deleted at the latest 7 days after the Event starts, or 7 days after Strava last refreshed it. The Event itself stays, so your order history can still show it, named by cafe and date.
  • An Event's route line: deleted 24 hours after the Event starts, or 6 days after Strava last refreshed it.
  • Your clubs: checked with Strava at least every 6 days and when you sync. When you leave a club on Strava, you leave it on Krew Cups at the next check. If we can't check your clubs with Strava for 7 days, your club memberships are removed until a check succeeds. A club name nobody has refreshed for 7 days is deleted.
  • Your Strava connection (athlete ID and access keys): while you're connected.
  • Photos attached to a reported problem: deleted 120 days after the report is filed, or when you delete your account.
  • Your account, Usual, Pickup name, whether you're going, order history, reports and suspensions: until you delete your account.
  • Payment records of past orders, and what a member owed: kept for 5 years after you delete your account, without your name or anything linking them to you, for the cafe's and our payment and tax records. Then deleted.
7 Withdrawing consent: removing Krew Cups from Strava Remove Krew Cups under My Apps in Strava. We delete your Strava data; your account stays.

You can stop Krew Cups reading your Strava data at any time, on Strava:

  1. Open Strava's settings → My Apps (https://www.strava.com/settings/apps).
  2. Find Krew Cups and choose Revoke Access.

Strava tells us straight away. We then delete your Strava connection and keys and your club memberships, and cancel, without charge, any order not yet sent to a cafe. An order already sent to the cafe still goes ahead. Your account, Usual, saved card, Pickup name and order history stay, so you can reconnect later and carry on. Since Krew Cups needs Strava, the app will ask you to reconnect next time you open it.

We do the same if Strava stops letting us access your account, for example because you deleted your Strava account.

8 Deleting your account Settings → Delete account, or email us. In the app it happens at once; by email within 30 days.

To delete your Krew Cups account, open Settings → Delete account in the app and confirm. You can also email info@krewcups.com and ask us to do it. We'll do it within 30 days.

When you delete your account, at once:

  • we revoke Krew Cups' access to your Strava account, so it disappears from your Strava connected apps;
  • we delete your customer record and saved card from Stripe;
  • we cancel, without charge, any order not yet sent to a cafe;
  • we delete your Usual, Pickup name, whether you're going to Events, tracked positions, distances, Strava connection, club memberships, menu prices you entered, push tokens, notification history and suspensions;
  • we blank the notes you added to orders and what you wrote in reports, and delete your photos;
  • we delete your sign-in account and email address;
  • last, we email you to confirm your account and data are deleted.

If one of your orders is already with the cafe and not yet finished, we can't delete your account until it is. The app tells you when this is the case.

Past orders, reports, payment records and anything a member owed are kept, because the cafe and Krew Cups need them for their payment and tax records. They no longer link to you and don't carry your name: they show as "Deleted member". They're deleted 5 years later.

If you set up a Stripe payout account to place group orders, it belongs to you and stays with Stripe. Close it with Stripe directly.

The confirmation email comes from no-reply@activation.krewcups.com. Replies go to info@krewcups.com.

9 Your rights See, correct, copy, delete or limit your data, wherever you live. Email us.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you, in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use.

Email info@krewcups.com. We'll reply within 30 days. You can change your Pickup name, Usual and card yourself in the app, and switch location and notifications off in your phone's settings.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, email us first. If we can't resolve it and you live outside Australia, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

10 Security Each member reads only their own data. Strava keys are encrypted. We'll tell you about a breach.

Your data is stored with Supabase, and each member can only read their own data. Strava access keys are kept encrypted in a separate vault. Card details are held by Stripe, never by us. Only Rudi Jooste, who runs Krew Cups, can access the data behind the app.

If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you promptly. If it involves Strava data, we'll also tell Strava within 24 hours.
