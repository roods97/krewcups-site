5. How Orders and payments work

5.1 These are the payment rules for every Order. Changing them needs a new version of these terms.

5.2 Payment rules:

  • (a) Charging. The Member is charged only when your staff move the Order to Making. Before that, nothing is charged and the Member may change or cancel the Order for free.
  • (b) Orders never started. An Order your staff never move to Making is never charged.
  • (c) Failed payment. If the charge fails, the Order is marked "don't make". You may retry it or Reject it. Don't make it unless the charge succeeds.
  • (d) Reject. You may Reject any Order, with a reason. If it was already charged, the Member automatically gets a full refund from your Stripe account, including the Platform fee.
  • (e) Fallbacks. If you can't make the Order as placed, you may only apply the Member's own pre-approved Fallback, or Reject. The Member pays the lower of the two prices. If the Fallback is cheaper, the difference is refunded from your account. If the Fallback is dearer, the Member pays nothing extra and you absorb the difference.
  • (f) No-show. If the Member doesn't collect a Ready Order, it becomes a No-show and you keep the money. This is final unless a Flag later results in a refund.
  • (g) Prices are fixed. An Order is charged at the price the Member saw when they placed it. A later change to your menu doesn't affect Orders already placed.
  • (h) Cancelled rides. If a club cancels a ride before it starts, its Orders are cancelled before anything is charged.

5.3 The Order guide. The timings and screens of the order flow (when an Order arrives on your board, when it becomes a No-show, and so on) are described in the Order guide at https://krewcups.com/order-guide/. We may update the Order guide with 14 days' notice by email. An update to the Order guide can never move money away from you; that needs a new version of these terms.

5.4 Arrival estimates aren't guaranteed. Order timing depends on Members' estimated arrival, which may be wrong. Krew Cups doesn't guarantee estimates, and an Order that becomes a No-show stays yours (clause 5.2(f)), even if an estimate was wrong.

9. Flags

9.1 What a Flag is. A Flag is a report that something went wrong with one Order. A Member may Flag a Collected or No-show Order, and you may Flag a Collected, Rejected or No-show Order. Either side may Flag only within 24 hours of the Order's arrival estimate.

9.2 Your response to a Member's Flag. You'll see the Member's reason, description and photo. You must respond within 2 Working days with one of:

  • (a) Refund: any amount up to the Order's menu price, from your Stripe account. The Platform fee stays with Krew Cups;
  • (b) Contest with Krew Cups: the app was at fault, not you. Add a note for the Operator;
  • (c) Contest with Member: you got the Order right. Add a note. The Member then accepts or contests back; or
  • (d) Resolved in person: you already sorted it at the counter. Add a note saying what you did. The Member then confirms or contests back.

9.3 If the Member doesn't answer your response within 2 Working days, they're taken to accept it.

9.4 Reminders. We'll show the Flag and the time left on your board, and email you when it arrives and again about 12 hours before the deadline.

9.5 The automatic refund. The Platform fee stays with Krew Cups. This applies only to Orders sent to you after you accepted a version of these terms that includes this clause.

9.6 The Operator. A Flag that is contested goes to the Krew Cups Operator, who reviews it and closes it as Upheld, Not upheld or Fee refunded, aiming to do so within 5 Working days. If the Operator needs more information, they'll email you. The Operator can't refund your money. After an Upheld decision, you may still Refund at any time.
