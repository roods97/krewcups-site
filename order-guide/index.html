How Krew Cups Orders reach your board, what each step does, and when things happen on their own. "You" means your Cafe. The money rules (when a Member is charged, refunds, No-shows) are set by the Cafe terms, clause 5.2. This guide explains how they work in practice and never changes them.

At a glance

  1. 10 min before the Member's Arrival estimate The Order arrives in Incoming. Nothing is charged yet.
  2. You move it to Making The Member is charged. The Order is locked in.
  3. You tap Ready, then Collected Collected takes it off the board. The Member can also tap Collected in the app.
  4. 30 min after Ready (or after the Arrival estimate, if that's later) A Ready Order nobody collected becomes a No-show. The Member stays charged.
  5. 30 min after the Arrival estimate An Order you never started leaves the board, Not made. It's never charged.
  6. 2 hours after an Order turns Overdue An Order still in Making is moved to Collected.
  7. 24 hours after the Arrival estimate The last chance for either side to Flag the Order.
  8. 2 Working days after a Flag arrives Your deadline to respond. We remind you about 12 hours before.

Your board

Your board has three columns: Incoming, Making and Ready. Each card shows the Member's Pickup name, the drink and size, and any Changes (such as Oat milk, Extra shot or Dine in). A standard drink shows just its size and name. Write the Pickup name on the cup exactly as shown.

Cards count down to the Member's Arrival estimate: when they're expected to walk in. That's when the drink should be ready. In Incoming and Making, identical drinks share one card that lists every Pickup name on it. In Ready, each Order gets its own card for handover.

Keep the board open during service, so you see Orders as they arrive.

When Orders arrive

  • About 10 minutes out. An Order lands in Incoming 10 minutes before the Member's Arrival estimate. Krew Cups follows each Member's progress on the ride or run, so a fast Member's Order arrives earlier and a slow one's later.
  • Late order. A Member who skipped or missed their Order can still place one until 5 minutes after their Arrival estimate. If it's already past the 10-minute mark, it goes straight to Incoming.
  • Held order. If a Member ordered ahead but we can't see they're out with the rest of the ride, we hold the Order and ask them if they still want it. A Held order isn't on your board. If they say yes, or we see them arrive, it reaches Incoming straight away. If they cancel, or don't answer by 5 minutes after their Arrival estimate, it's cancelled and you never see it.
  • Cancelled rides. If a Club cancels a ride before it starts, its Orders are cancelled before they reach you.

Incoming

Nothing in Incoming has been charged. Until you start it, the Member can still change or cancel their Order: the card updates, or disappears if they cancel.

Moving a card to Making charges the Member. Only start an Order when you're about to make it. Two things can stop it moving:

  • "Order changed — read it again". The Member changed their Order just as you moved it. It stays in Incoming, uncharged. Read the card again, then move it to Making.
  • "Payment failed — don't make". The Member's card was declined. It stays in Incoming and the Member is asked to update their card. Don't make it. Move it to Making again to retry the charge, or Reject it.

Edit (for something that's run out) and Reject work in Incoming too, just as in Making.

Making

The Member has paid and the Order is theirs. There's no Undo from Making. If you moved one by mistake, Reject it, and the Member is refunded.

  • Something's run out. Use Edit. You can only apply the Member's own Fallback, the swap they approved in advance. If they have none that you can make, Reject the Order.
  • Reject. Choose a reason. The Member is told straight away and, once the Order was charged, refunded in full.
  • Overdue. A Making card is marked Overdue once the Arrival estimate (as it stood when the Order reached your board) has passed and it isn't Ready yet. It's just a nudge: tap Ready, hand it over, or Reject it.

Ready and Collected

Tap Ready when the drink is on the counter. Ready cards count the minutes since the drink was made. When you hand it over, tap Collected. The Member can also tap Collected in their app, which takes the card off your board too. If you tap Ready or Collected by mistake, use Undo.

No-show. If a Member doesn't collect a Ready Order, it becomes a No-show 30 minutes after the later of when you tapped Ready and their Arrival estimate (as it stood when the Order reached your board). If you know they aren't coming, tap No-show on the Ready card. A No-show is final: the Member stays charged and nothing is refunded, unless a Flag later leads to one.

Orders that leave on their own

  • Not made. An Order still in Incoming 30 minutes after the Member's Arrival estimate (the latest one, as its countdown shows) leaves the board. It's never charged, and the Member is told it wasn't made. This includes "Payment failed" Orders.
  • Left in Making. An Order still in Making 2 hours after it turned Overdue is moved to Collected quietly. Nothing is charged or refunded. If the Member never got it, they can Flag it.
  • No-show. A Ready Order nobody collected, as above.

Every Order, however it ended, stays in your Service log.

Flags

A Flag is a report that something went wrong with one Order. Either side can Flag an Order until 24 hours after the Member's Arrival estimate (as it stood when the Order reached your board).

When a Member Flags an Order

A Member can Flag a Collected or No-show Order. You'll see their reason, what went wrong and a photo. A banner on your board shows how many Flags are waiting and the time left. We also email you when a Flag arrives and again about 12 hours before your deadline, at your Cafe account email (until you have a Cafe account, the email on your Stripe account).

Give your Cafe response within 2 Working days. It's one of:

  • Refund: any amount up to the Order's menu price.
  • Contest with Krew Cups: the app was at fault, not you. Add a note for us.
  • Contest with Member: you got the Order right. Add a note.
  • Resolved in person: you already sorted it at the counter. Say what you did.

After a Contest with Member or Resolved in person, the Member has 2 Working days to accept or contest back. If they don't answer, they're taken to accept. A contested Flag comes to Krew Cups to review; we aim to close it within 5 Working days.

What happens if you don't respond in time is set by the Cafe terms (clause 9.5): please don't let a Flag run out.

Flagging an Order yourself

You can Flag a Collected, Rejected or No-show Order from its row in your Service log, within the same 24 hours: for example, the Member didn't collect, behaved badly at the counter, or the Order was wrong when it reached you. A photo is optional. Your Flag comes straight to Krew Cups, and the Member never sees it.

Working days

A Working day is Monday to Friday on Perth time, excluding Western Australian public holidays. That applies wherever your Cafe is.

The clock only runs on Working days, to the minute. A Flag that arrives on Wednesday at 9:14 am is due on Friday at 9:14 am. One that arrives on Friday at 3 pm is due on Tuesday at 3 pm. One that arrives at the weekend starts counting at midnight on Monday, so it's due at the end of Tuesday. A public holiday is skipped like a weekend day.

Changes to this guide

We may update this guide with 14 days' notice by email. An update can never move money away from you: that needs a new version of the Cafe terms. Questions? Email info@krewcups.com.
