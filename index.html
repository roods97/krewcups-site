How it works

You set it up once. After that, every club ride or run on Strava can end with a coffee.

  1. 01

    Save your usual

    Drink, size, milk, shots, extras. Connect Strava and your payment card, and you're set.

  2. 02

    Strava finds your session

    Krew Cups watches your Strava clubs for upcoming rides and runs. Before each one you get a nudge: order your usual, tweak it, or skip. Event cancelled on Strava, or didn't show up? No order placed.

  3. 03

    Coffee ready when you arrive

    Krew Cups estimates your arrival from your Strava route and pace, and places your order when you're about 10 minutes away. The cafe makes it, and you collect it by name.
